Privacy

Privacy policy

How we handle the information you share when you contact us or request a rental. We keep this short and plain.

Last updated: October 12, 2026

What we collect

We only collect what you give us through our forms and messages:

  • Contact details you enter: name, email, and phone number
  • Location details you enter: city and ZIP code (to confirm we deliver to you)
  • Anything you type into a message, notes, or preferences field
  • Basic, non-identifying website usage data unless you opt out of analytics (see below)

We do not ask for payment card numbers, Social Security numbers, or account passwords on this website.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your questions
  • To process a reservation request or waitlist entry and confirm delivery availability
  • To arrange delivery, installation, training, support, and pickup for a rental
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the site is used so we can improve it

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the business, and only as needed to do so:

  • Netlify, which hosts this website and stores our form submissions
  • Resend, which sends our transactional and notification emails
  • Twilio, which delivers our admin text-message alerts
  • ImprovMX, which forwards email sent to our addresses
  • Google Analytics, unless you opt out, and configured so it does not receive your name, email, phone, address, ZIP, city, or the contents of your messages
  • Meta (Facebook), through its pixel, unless you opt out, so we can measure whether our Facebook and Instagram ads led to a request, and show our ads again to past visitors. It doesn't receive your name, email, phone, address, ZIP, city, or the contents of your messages.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

All of the above excludes text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes, and mobile numbers, SMS opt-in data, and SMS consent information will not be shared with any third parties except Twilio, our SMS delivery provider, and only to deliver the messages.

Analytics and cookies

Purebaric operates and advertises only in Utah. To understand site traffic and measure the performance of our advertising, we use Google Analytics 4, which is on by default. You can turn it off on this device at any time with the button below; turning it off does not affect your ability to use the site or submit a request. We do not send any personal or health information to analytics.

We honor Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track. If your browser sends either signal, or you turn off analytics on this device, Google Analytics and the Meta (Facebook) pixel don't load at all.

To learn which ads, searches, or websites bring people to us, this site also keeps a note in your own browser (local storage) of how you first arrived: the campaign tags and ad click codes in the link you followed, the website that sent you, the first page you saw, and when. It holds no name, contact details, address, or health information, and it expires after 90 days. It reaches us only if you send a request, join the waitlist, or send us a message, together with that submission. With a request, we also note whether you used a phone or a computer. It isn't created or sent when your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, or when you turn off analytics or personalization, and turning off analytics also erases it.

While analytics is enabled, Google Analytics may store anonymous browser, session, and advertising click identifiers. We keep the anonymous browser and session identifiers with a private reservation record so a later confirmed rental can be connected to the original website visit. We share conversion events (such as an accepted request or confirmed rental) with Purebaric's linked Google Ads account so we can measure whether an advertisement led to a rental. We may use the Meta pixel to show our own ads again, on Facebook and Instagram, to people who've visited our site. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, or you turn off analytics on this device, the pixel doesn't load and you won't be included. Purebaric does not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, ZIP codes, health information, notes, or form contents to Google Analytics or Google Ads.

SMS privacy

Purebaric uses Twilio to send transactional website-notification text messages only to the Purebaric owner or another authorized administrator after that person affirmatively opts in through the password-protected admin portal. Customers and the public are never sent messages through this program. The opt-in process is documented at purebaric.com/sms-consent.

What we collect for SMS: the administrator's mobile number, the notification categories they selected, the date, time and identity of the person who gave consent, and message-delivery status from Twilio.

How we use it: solely to send the notification categories the administrator selected (new reservation requests, out-of-area requests, new reviews, and a weekly rentals-ending reminder). Message frequency varies with website activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Sharing: No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell, rent, or share mobile numbers, SMS opt-in data, or SMS consent information with any third parties, other than Twilio as our delivery provider acting on our instructions.

Opting out: reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts, or turn off SMS notifications in the admin portal. Reply HELP, email hello@purebaric.com or call (801) 901-0873 for help. Consent is optional and is not a condition of any purchase or service.

Twilio processes message-delivery information as a service provider to Purebaric. SMS consent records are retained only as needed to operate the notification service, document consent, comply with applicable requirements, and honor opt-out requests. See also the SMS terms in our Terms of Service.

Homepage personalization

To show you the chamber you looked at when you come back, this site keeps a small record in your own browser (local storage, not a cookie). It notes which chamber type or business rental you showed interest in (including through one of our ads), which of our Utah city pages you read (we go by the page you read, not your location), the rental length you last picked, whether you've recently sent a request, your visit count, and a randomly assigned group. It holds no name, contact details, ZIP code, address, or health information, and it expires after 45 days without a visit. The record itself stays in your browser. From it, Google Analytics receives only this, like other site events: on the homepage, which version you saw (for example, "sitting chamber") and why (an ad, pages you viewed, or a city page), whether a city line or reminder showed, and whether it's your first or a return visit; on every page, whether you're in the personalized group or the comparison group, the 1 in 10 visitors who always see the standard homepage. If you send a request, we also note on it which of those two groups you were in. None of this is kept or sent if you turn off analytics or personalization, or if your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed to answer your inquiry or support your rental relationship, and as required for our records. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email hello@purebaric.com or call (801) 901-0873 and we'll help.

A note on health information

Purebaric provides general wellness equipment and cannot give personalized medical advice. Please do not enter sensitive medical details in our free-text fields. For medical questions, talk with your physician.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above reflects the latest version. Questions about your privacy? Email hello@purebaric.com.

This summary is provided for transparency and is not legal advice.
