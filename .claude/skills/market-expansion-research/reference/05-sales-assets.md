# Phases 5 and 6. Channel proposals and sales assets

All generators live in `_internal/outreach/source/`. Fonts, logos and branded chamber photos are in the site repo (`images/logo.png`, `images/logo-cream.png`, `_internal/outreach/branded-images/`).

## 1. Letter of intent (state version)
`make_loi.js` holds a `STATES` object. Add an entry for the new state: tax clause (no rate, no "each invoice" until the local-portion question is answered), `airOnly: true` for a new state, conditions (registrations for the contracting entity; licences only if there is a local base; Purebaric obtains the fire official's written answer for the installation's jurisdiction; counsel confirms no state or federal requirement bars the air-only rental; site check; equipment availability), the conditions note, governing law and venue (the new state's law and the county's courts for the LOI itself; the Definitive Agreement keeps its own clause), time zone, counsel wording, footer and `contact` (the local contact details from intake). Run with `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node make_loi.js '{"<state>":"<out.docx>"}'`, export the PDF through Word, render and look at every page, extract the text and confirm the contact details and the absence of the Standard oxygen option. Write `legal/README-LOI-<State>.md` (what it does, differences from Utah, before-sending checklist including the contracting entity and the state version of the rental agreement).

## 2. Pamphlets, one per industry
`make_pamphlets_market.py` takes a JSON config (market name, state, contact phone/email/web, air-only flag, local price anchor rows and footnote, current business rates, the "coming to" sentence, tax note, industry overrides). Copy `pamphlet_config_knoxville.json`, edit, run with the scratch Python environment that has fpdf2 and Pillow (`python3 -m venv <scratch>/venv && <scratch>/venv/bin/pip install fpdf2 pillow pypdf`). Seven trifolds: gyms; CrossFit and performance; sports clubs and teams; athletic departments (pilot panel instead of revenue panel); recovery studios, IV lounges and med spas; chiropractic and wellness practices; salons and day spas. Then render pages and look at them, and extract the text with pypdf and grep for: the contact details (twice per pamphlet), hello@, any other phone number, the home state's name, "therapy", "HBOT", "patients", "treat", "heal", "inflammation", "sleep", "detox", "immunity", "this month", "only one", "first in". Fix and regenerate until clean. Pamphlet arithmetic: tiers 1, 2 and 4 sessions a day at $75; worked example on two a day; rental at the 12-month rate; $1.30 supplies; label the result "after rental and supplies, before tax, card fees and staff time". Price anchors come from the provider workbook and say what they are (oxygen-enriched sessions at a chiropractic office, in Knoxville's case).

## 7. Conversions and checks
- Markdown to Word: `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node md2docx.js in.md out.docx "Footer text"` (headings, bullets, numbered lists, pipe tables with content-sized columns, bold and italic).
- Word to PDF: osascript to Microsoft Word (`open`, `save as ... file format format PDF`, `close saving no`), with a timeout of 120 seconds; the app must be installed.
- PDF to PNG: the `pdf2png` Swift helper (PDFKit) built in the scratch folder: `swiftc -O pdf2png.swift -o pdf2png`, then `./pdf2png file.pdf prefix`.
- PDF text: pypdf in the scratch venv.
- Keep generators in `_internal/outreach/source/` and commit them with the outputs.
