# Agent prompt templates

Rules for every prompt: `<path>` is always the absolute Desktop research folder (for example `/Users/bailie/Desktop/Boise Research/boise`); never give a subagent a relative path or a reference to a skill file, because it starts in the Adobe OneDrive working directory and cannot see this skill. Paste rubrics, topic lists and schemas into the prompt. Every prompt carries: the Purebaric description, the task, the absolute output path and exact keys, the search budget, the no-fabrication rule, the rewrite-every-N-records rule, and the report format. Replace angle-bracket fields.

**Purebaric description (paste into every prompt):** "Purebaric (purebaric.com) is a Utah company that rents soft-shell mild hyperbaric chambers (1.3 to 1.5 ATA, air compressor; an electric oxygen concentrator is optional and is not placed in new states until counsel and the fire marshal have answered) to homes and to businesses that sell sessions to their clients, as a general-wellness amenity with no prescription. Research only; never fabricate a fact about a real business or person; mark anything unverified."

**Industry strings (paste where rows are produced):** "Gyms and fitness centers", "CrossFit and performance gyms", "Sports teams and athletic programs", "High schools and colleges (athletics)", "Recovery studios and med spas", "Physical therapy, chiropractic, and sports medicine", "Salons and beauty studios".

**Row schema (paste where rows are produced):** exactly these 15 keys, "" when unknown: business, industry, owner, owner_title, email, email_confidence ("high" = printed on the business's own site or official listing, "medium" = third-party directory, "none"), email_source, phone, website, address, city, zip, county, notes, personal_note_hook. Output is one JSON object `{"prospects": [...], "already_has_hbot": [...]}`. An email counts only if it appears verbatim in a search result or page; never construct one. Hooks only when verifiable. A business that already offers hyperbaric goes in `already_has_hbot`.
