# Phases 5 and 6. Channel proposals and sales assets

All generators live in `_internal/outreach/source/`. Fonts, logos and branded chamber photos are in the site repo (`images/logo.png`, `images/logo-cream.png`, `_internal/outreach/branded-images/`).

## 1. Letter of intent (state version)
`make_loi.js` holds a `STATES` object. Add an entry for the new state with every key: `name`, `short`, `title`, `taxClause` (no rate, no "each invoice" until the local-portion question is answered), `airOnly: false` and `renewal: true` where the state has a renewal statute (the configuration line is the standard package for every state; the insurer gate for minors uses `st.name`), `conditions` (registrations for the contracting entity; licences only if there is a local base; the business confirms lease and zoning and obtains landlord consent; local delivery, service and insurance capacity; site check; equipment availability; counsel confirms no state or federal requirement bars the rental) and `conditionsNote` re-lettered to match your own list, `lawClause` (the new state's law and the county's courts for the LOI itself), `time`, `counsel`, `foot`, `contact` (the local contact details from intake) and `entity` (the contracting entity from intake, else the default d/b/a line). Pass the date and live prices from the environment: `LOI_DATE="<date>" PRICING_JSON='<the /api/pricing JSON>' NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node make_loi.js '{"<state>":"<out.docx>"}'`. Export the PDF through Word, render and look at every page, then run the LOI scan in `tooling.md`. Write `legal/README-LOI-<State>.md` (what it does, differences from Utah, before-sending checklist including the contracting entity and the state version of the rental agreement).

## 1a. State version of the rental agreement and the home waiver
`make_waiver.js` generates the Business Rental Agreement and Liability Waiver and takes a `STATE` environment variable (`STATE=tennessee WAIVER_OUT=<path> node make_waiver.js`). Add a state entry with: the local contact details, the contracting entity, the standard-package Exhibit A, governing law and venue as counsel directs, the state's automatic-renewal and recurring-charge section (section 2.7 in the Tennessee version), and the release wording ("under applicable law"). The home rental agreement and waiver are not in the repo; the owner holds them. Produce `Purebaric_<State>_Automatic_Renewal_Disclosure.docx` (from `make_renewal_rider.js`) as a rider the owner attaches to the home documents and shows in the booking flow until the home documents themselves are revised, and list that as an owner action.

## 2. Pamphlets, one per industry
Owner's rule (10 Oct 2026): the pamphlets are the Utah facility pamphlet with only the key details changed, not a new design. Use `make_pamphlets.py <market> [out_dir]` (it is `make_pamphlet2.py` in a loop over seven genres, with a `MARKETS` dict): add a market entry with the contact details, local line, price anchors and footnote, and keep the owner's wording everywhere else. Business genres keep the title "A New Revenue-Generating Amenity for Your Business"; the two team genres (sports teams and clubs; high school and college athletics) use "Help Your Athletes Recover Faster", a "Why It Helps Your Team" panel, a cost panel instead of the revenue panel, and "Who uses the chamber?". Every genre has its own "Who books sessions?" rows. The injury list in the team panel is a health-adjacent claim: it needs the qualified reviewer sign-off that RUNBOOK section 14 requires before printing. The config-driven `make_pamphlets_market.py` is the older, more heavily rewritten version; do not use it unless the owner asks for different copy.
`make_pamphlets_market.py <config.json> <out_dir>` takes a JSON config with exactly these keys: `market`, `state`, `state_abbrev`, `air_only`, `contact_phone`, `contact_email`, `contact_web`, `business_rates` (`{"3": <3-month rate>, "12": <12-month rate>}` from `/api/pricing`), `price_anchors` (list of `[label, value, note]` rows from the provider workbook), `price_anchor_footnote`, `coming_line`, and optionally `assumption_vs_local` (the clause after "at $75 a session"; the default says "below the local single-session price", so change it if the lowest local anchor is $75 or less). Industry copy is the `INDUSTRIES` dict inside the script; edit it there if a market needs different wording. Copy `pamphlet_config_knoxville.json`, edit, and always pass the output folder as the second argument. Run with the scratch Python environment (`tooling.md`). Seven trifolds: gyms; CrossFit and performance; sports clubs and teams; athletic departments (pilot panel instead of revenue panel); recovery studios, IV lounges and med spas; chiropractic and wellness practices; salons and day spas. Then render pages and look at them, and run the pamphlet scan in `tooling.md` until it is clean. Pamphlet arithmetic: tiers 1, 2 and 4 sessions a day at $75; worked example on two a day; rental at the 12-month rate; $1.30 supplies; label the result "after rental and supplies, before tax, card fees and staff time". Price anchors come from the provider workbook and say what they are (oxygen-enriched sessions at a chiropractic office, in Knoxville's case).
