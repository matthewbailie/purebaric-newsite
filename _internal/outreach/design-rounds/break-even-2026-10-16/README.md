# Break-even module, design round of 16 October 2026

Six treatments of the "break even at 11 sessions a month" block that flows into the revenue table on the inside middle panel of the business pamphlets. `variants.py` renders them (run with `~/.venvs/purebaric/bin/python variants.py <out_dir>`).

- A: large numeral and label, rule, table.
- B: terracotta header band attached to the table.
- C: session scale with markers at 11 and 104.
- D: net figure first, break-even as a pill.
- E: dark band with the numeral, connector, plain rows, large net figure (built from the critiques).
- F: E with the typographer's fixes. **Shipped** in `make_pamphlets.py`.

Three critics (print and brand designer, sales-collateral specialist, typographer) reviewed A to D. Where they agreed: break-even must come before the net figure; numerals in Helvetica Bold because Georgia's are old-style figures; cut the explanatory sentence; cut "one-year lease" from the table row; "Net each month" set large; any band in dark terracotta with bold text 9 pt or larger; rules 0.75 pt terracotta inset to the text edge; add "About 4 sessions a day, 6 days a week" so the 104 is explained.
