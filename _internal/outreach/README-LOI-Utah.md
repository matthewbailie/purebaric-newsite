# Letter of Intent, business chamber rental: Utah version

Files: `Purebaric_LOI_Business_Chamber_Rental_Utah.docx` (editable) and `.pdf` (send-ready). Generator: `source/make_loi.js` (run with `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node make_loi.js '{"utah":"out.docx"}'`). Drafted 10 Oct 2026 as a template for review by a Utah attorney; it is not legal advice.

## What it does
A non-binding term sheet that leads into `purebaric_business_rental_agreement_and_liability_waiver.docx` (the "Definitive Agreement"). Only Section 7 binds: confidentiality (two years), each side's own costs, no announcements or advertising before installation, a wellness-only and no-revenue-reliance acknowledgment, independent-contractor status (no franchise), Utah law and Utah County venue (matching the Definitive Agreement), and counterparts. Sections 1 to 4 and 6 are intent only: equipment and configuration (standard with concentrator, or air-only), number of chambers, term and target date, fees from Schedule 1 plus the $200 training and supplies fee, Utah sales tax on rentals, services included, business responsibilities (space, trained staff, consent forms, $1M/$2M insurance naming Purebaric, no medical claims, landlord and zoning check), who may use the chamber, 30-day termination, the seven steps to installation, four conditions, an optional chamber hold with a fully refundable deposit, and a 30-day expiry.

## Use
1. Send after the prospectus conversation, when the business wants to move but is not ready to sign the full agreement. Fill Section 1.1 to 1.3 with them, or leave the checkboxes for them to tick.
2. Keep Schedule 1 in step with purebaric.com/pricing; the letter says the published rates on the signing date govern and are then fixed for the initial term.
3. Section 4 (reservation) is optional. Use it when inventory is tight; leave both boxes unchecked otherwise.
4. The under-18 rule (parent signs and is present) matches the Definitive Agreement. Utah does not enforce a parent's pre-injury release of a child's claims (*Hawkins v. Peart*, Utah 2001), so insurance and supervision, not the waiver, protect against claims by minors.
5. The Tennessee version in `research/knoxville-tennessee/legal/` adds Tennessee tax, registration and fire-code conditions and uses Tennessee law for the LOI.
