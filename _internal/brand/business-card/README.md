# Purebaric business card

`Purebaric_Business_Card_print.pdf` is print-ready: two pages (front, back), 3.5 x 2 in finished size with a 0.125 in bleed on every side (3.75 x 2.25 in page), all text and the QR code inside the 0.125 in safe zone. Front: cream, the wordmark, a terracotta rule and the one-line promise. Back: ink, the mark, name, title, phone, email, website, city, and a QR code to purebaric.com. Regenerate with `make_business_card.py` after changing the contact details.

**Where to order:** MOO (moo.com), the "Original" or "Luxe" business cards, 16pt or 32pt stock, matte finish, standard size. Upload the PDF as a two-page file, front then back, and choose "I have bleed included". MOO prints the cream and ink flat and true, which cheaper printers often shift. Budget alternative: Vistaprint standard matte, same upload, expect slightly warmer cream. Order 100 to start; the Tennessee version needs Michael's details and a fresh file.
