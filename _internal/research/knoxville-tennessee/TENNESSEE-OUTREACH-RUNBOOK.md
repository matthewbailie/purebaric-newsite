# Tennessee outreach runbook: proof of concept for business and school chamber rentals in the Knoxville area

Version 1, 10 October 2026. Scope: placing rented mild hyperbaric chambers at businesses and athletic programs within about 25 miles of downtown Knoxville, without billboards or paid marketing. Goal of the phase: prove that Knoxville businesses will rent and that their clients will buy sessions, at a cost and risk small enough to walk away from. Companion documents: `SALES-REP-TRAINING.md` (scripts), `knoxville/legal_blockers_summary.md` (what must be cleared first), `knoxville/legal/Purebaric_LOI_Business_Chamber_Rental_Tennessee.docx`, `knoxville/purebaric_knoxville_prospects.xlsx` (the list), `channel-schools-athletic-recovery/PROPOSAL-schools-athletic-recovery.md`.

---

## 1. Proof-of-concept definition

**Success looks like this within 120 days of the first outreach call:**

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Signed LOIs | 6 |
| Installed placements | 3 (at least two different industries) |
| Sessions per chamber per month, by month 2 | 10 or more |
| Renewals at the end of the first term | 2 of 3 |
| Fully loaded cost of the phase (freight, local labour, legal, travel) | Under $15,000 |
| Incidents, complaints or regulatory contact | Zero |

**Walk-away triggers.** Counsel says home or business rental needs a licence Purebaric cannot reasonably hold; the Knoxville Fire Marshal rules that air-only soft chambers are hyperbaric facilities under IFC §609; fewer than three LOIs after 60 days of full-time outreach; or insurance cannot be bound for Tennessee.

**What is deliberately out of scope.** Home rentals in Tennessee (blocked pending a home-medical-equipment licence decision), billboards and paid ads, a Knoxville warehouse, and any placement that asks for medical claims or prescription use.

## 2. Phase 0: gates to clear before any outreach (weeks 1 to 4)

Nothing is sent to a business until the first four items are done. The rest run in parallel.

1. **Legal opinion.** Engage a Tennessee attorney (health-care regulatory, Knoxville or Nashville). Send the two memos and ask the twelve questions in `legal_state_tennessee.md` section (d) and `legal_federal_local_knoxville.md` section (d). The three answers that gate everything: whether renting Rx-labelled devices to a business is "wholesale distribution" needing a Board of Pharmacy licence; whether air-only placements change the analysis; and whether the Definitive Agreement's release survives *Copeland* in Tennessee.
2. **Fire marshal inquiry, in writing.** Email the City of Knoxville Fire Prevention Bureau and the Knox County Fire Prevention Bureau with a one-page description of the soft chamber (1.3 to 1.5 ATA, air-only, no stored oxygen) and ask questions 4 to 7 from the federal/local memo: is it a "hyperbaric facility" under IFC 2024 §609 in a gym or studio, what is required if so, and is any permit or inspection triggered. Keep the reply in the legal file. Ask Maryville and Oak Ridge only if a prospect there reaches the LOI stage.
3. **Configuration decision.** Decide, with counsel, what is offered in Tennessee during the pilot. Default: air-only for businesses and schools. If counsel and the fire marshal clear the concentrator, add it as an upgrade later.
4. **Insurance.** Ask the current carrier or a broker to confirm general and product liability coverage for Tennessee placements, air-only and standard, and for use by minors under supervision. Get it in writing. Without this, schools and clubs are off the list.
5. **Registrations.** Tennessee Secretary of State certificate of authority for the LLC ($300 minimum); TNTAP registration for sales and use tax and business tax; franchise and excise tax registration. City of Knoxville and Knox County business licences ($15 each) are needed only once there is a local base or stock. Decide whether the representative's home counts as a base (ask counsel; keep stock off-site in a storage unit if that avoids it).
6. **Local presence.** An 865 phone number forwarding to the representative; a Knoxville service-area entry on the Google Business Profile; a "Knoxville" line on the business page of the website that says "coming soon, by invitation" and nothing more; the facility pamphlet reprinted with Tennessee wording ("recovery session", tax line, Knoxville contact).
7. **Documents.** Tennessee LOI (done), Definitive Agreement reviewed by Tennessee counsel, client consent form with Tennessee wording, one-page site requirements, one-page "for your landlord and fire marshal" description, Tennessee pricing sheet with tax.
8. **Representative.** Hire or contract one Knoxville-based representative (commission plus day rate) who can also deliver, install and service. Train them on `SALES-REP-TRAINING.md`, a video install, and a session in a chamber. Ship one chamber to them for demonstrations and for their own familiarity.
9. **Equipment logistics.** Freight two chambers, compressors and accessory kits from Lehi to a climate-controlled Knoxville storage unit (10 by 10 is enough for four kits). Keep two in reserve for the first installs; order more only after the third LOI. Budget freight at the actual quote; do not estimate.
10. **Tracking.** Add Tennessee rows to the admin portal only when an agreement is signed; until then the prospect workbook is the system of record. Add a "Status", "Last contact" and "Next step" column to the Prospects sheet and update it daily.

## 3. Phase 1: who to contact first, and in what order (weeks 5 to 12)

The list has 210 prospects. Do not work it top to bottom. Work it in the order below, one wave at a time, and move to the next wave only when the current wave's named prospects have each had three touches (visit, call, email) or a decision.

**Ranking logic.** First, businesses whose customers already pay $75 or more for recovery and that have a named owner and a treatment room. Second, performance gyms with a coach on the floor. Third, sports-medicine practices and pro clubs, which need a medical lead's blessing. Fourth, colleges, which have trainers and budgets but slow approvals. Fifth, everything else. Schools go last because they cross a season, need insurer approval and need the health systems that employ their trainers. Anything with "Yes" in the Hyperbaric on website column is skipped.

**Geography.** Work in clusters so a day's visits are within a few miles: (A) West Knoxville and Bearden along Kingston Pike; (B) Farragut and Hardin Valley; (C) downtown, North Knoxville and Powell; (D) Maryville and Alcoa; (E) Oak Ridge and Clinton. Clusters A and B hold most of wave 1.

## 4. Phase 2: from interest to install (per prospect)

1. **Day 0, interest.** Send the Tennessee LOI and the agreement the same day. Log it.
2. **Day 1 to 7, site check.** Photos or a video walk-through: 4 by 8 feet clear, grounded outlet, no flame or heat source in the room, temperature control, access path for a 60 lb chamber bag and a compressor.
3. **Day 7 to 14, approvals.** Business sends its COI (additional insured: Matthew Bailie d/b/a Purebaric) and landlord consent. Business confirms lease, zoning and fire code. If the fire marshal's general answer from Phase 0 was conditional, the business asks its inspector in writing using the one-page description.
4. **Day 14, sign.** Definitive Agreement signed; first month plus $200 collected by Stripe or ACH; sales tax applied.
5. **Day 14 to 21, install.** Representative installs, trains two staff, runs a session with them, leaves the runbook, consent forms and pricing suggestion, completes the checklist, takes photos.
6. **Day 28, first check-in.** Session count, questions, menu wording check (no "therapy").
7. **Day 60, renewal conversation.** Session count, revenue, staff feedback, upgrade to a longer term.

## 6. Weekly report and decision points

**Week 4:** Phase 0 complete? If counsel or the fire marshal has blocked air-only business placements, stop and reassess before any outreach.

**Week 8:** First six LOIs targeted. Fewer than two means the pitch or the list is wrong; review objections before continuing.

**Week 12:** Three installs targeted. Review freight and labour cost per install against the $15,000 budget.

**Week 16 (day 120):** Score against Section 1. Decide: expand (second representative, concentrator upgrade if cleared, home rentals if licensed), hold (keep three placements, no new outreach), or exit (remove equipment, keep the list warm).

## 8. Rules that do not bend during the pilot

- No Tennessee placement until Phase 0 items 1 to 4 are done.
- Air-only unless counsel and the fire marshal clear the concentrator in writing.
- No home rentals in Tennessee.
- No under-18 use without the insurer's written confirmation and a parent present.
- "Recovery session" or "wellness session", never "therapy", on any menu, sign, post or pamphlet.
- Every regulatory comment from a landlord, insurer, inspector or official is reported the same day and filed.
- The prospect workbook is updated the day of every contact.
