# Tennessee outreach runbook: proof of concept for business and school chamber rentals in the Knoxville area

Version 2, 10 October 2026 (revised after the round-1 critique). Scope: placing rented mild hyperbaric chambers at businesses and athletic programs within about 25 miles of downtown Knoxville, without billboards or paid marketing. Goal of the phase: prove that Knoxville businesses will rent and that their clients will buy sessions, at a cost and risk small enough to walk away from. Companion documents: `SALES-REP-TRAINING.md` (scripts), `knoxville/legal_blockers_summary.md` (what must be cleared first), `knoxville/legal/Purebaric_LOI_Business_Chamber_Rental_Tennessee.docx`, `knoxville/purebaric_knoxville_prospects.xlsx` (the list), `channel-schools-athletic-recovery/PROPOSAL-schools-athletic-recovery.md`.

---

## 1. Proof-of-concept definition

**Success looks like this, counted from the first outreach call (day 0 of outreach, which follows Phase 0):**

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Signed LOIs by day 60 of outreach | 3 (6 by day 120) |
| Installed placements by day 120 of outreach | 3 (at least two different industries) |
| Sessions per chamber per month, by each placement's month 2 (scored at day 180) | 10 or more |
| Renewals at the end of the first term (scored at day 180) | 2 of 3 |
| Fully loaded cost of the phase (line items in Section 7; cap provisional until the counsel and freight quotes are in) | Under $15,000 |
| Incidents, complaints or regulatory contact | Zero |

**Walk-away triggers.** Counsel says business rental needs a licence Purebaric cannot reasonably hold; the Knoxville Fire Marshal rules that air-only soft chambers are hyperbaric facilities under IFC §609, or does not answer by the end of Phase 0; fewer than three LOIs after 60 days of full-time outreach; or insurance cannot be bound for Tennessee.

**What is deliberately out of scope.** Home rentals in Tennessee (blocked pending a home-medical-equipment licence decision), billboards and paid ads, a Knoxville warehouse, and any placement that asks for medical claims or prescription use.

## 2. Phase 0: gates to clear before any outreach (weeks 1 to 4)

Nothing is sent to a business until the first four items are done. The rest run in parallel.

1. **Legal opinion.** Engage a Tennessee attorney (health-care regulatory, Knoxville or Nashville). Get a fee quote in week 1. Send the two memos and ask the nine questions in `legal_state_tennessee.md` section (d) and the twelve in `legal_federal_local_knoxville.md` section (d). Settle the contracting party at the same time: Tennessee contracts, the COI additional-insured line and the registrations must all name one entity (Purebaric LLC or Michael Bailie d/b/a Purebaric), and today the agreement and LOI name the individual while the registration plan names the LLC. The three answers that gate everything: whether renting Rx-labelled devices to a business is "wholesale distribution" needing a Board of Pharmacy licence; whether air-only placements change the analysis; and whether the Definitive Agreement's release survives *Copeland* in Tennessee.
2. **Fire marshal inquiry, in writing.** Email the City of Knoxville Fire Prevention Bureau, the Knox County Fire Prevention Bureau and the Town of Farragut (which adopts its own codes and holds much of wave 1) with a one-page description of the soft chamber (1.3 to 1.5 ATA, air-only, no stored oxygen) and ask questions 4 to 7 from the federal/local memo: is it a "hyperbaric facility" under IFC 2024 §609 in a gym or studio, what is required if so, and is any permit or inspection triggered. Keep the replies in the legal file. No reply from a jurisdiction by the end of week 4 means no outreach in that jurisdiction until there is one. Ask Maryville and Oak Ridge only if a prospect there reaches the LOI stage.
3. **Configuration decision.** Decide, with counsel, what is offered in Tennessee during the pilot. Default: air-only for businesses and schools. If counsel and the fire marshal clear the concentrator, add it as an upgrade later.
4. **Insurance.** Ask the current carrier or a broker to confirm general and product liability coverage for Tennessee placements, air-only and standard, and for use by minors under supervision. Get it in writing. Without this, schools and youth programs are off the list and the schools pamphlet is used for colleges and adult athletes only.
5. **Registrations.** Tennessee Secretary of State certificate of authority for the LLC ($300 minimum); TNTAP registration for sales and use tax and business tax; franchise and excise tax registration. City of Knoxville and Knox County business licences ($15 each) are needed only once there is a local base or stock. Decide whether the representative's home counts as a base (ask counsel; keep stock off-site in a storage unit if that avoids it).
6. **Local presence.** An 865 phone number forwarding to the representative; a Knoxville service-area entry on the Google Business Profile; a "Knoxville" line on the business page of the website that says "coming soon, by invitation" and nothing more; the facility pamphlet reprinted with Tennessee wording ("recovery session", tax line, Knoxville contact).
7. **Documents.** Tennessee LOI (done), Definitive Agreement reviewed by Tennessee counsel, client consent form with Tennessee wording, one-page site requirements, one-page "for your landlord and fire marshal" description, Tennessee pricing sheet with tax.
8. **Representative.** Hire or contract one Knoxville-based representative (commission plus a stated day rate, written into the budget) who can also deliver, install and service, and name a backup service contact (a second contractor or Michael on a flight) so the agreement's two-business-day response can be met when the representative is unavailable. Train them on `SALES-REP-TRAINING.md`, a video install, and a session in a chamber. Ship one chamber to them for their own familiarization and staff demonstrations only; no customer sessions on it until Tennessee insurance is bound.
9. **Equipment logistics.** Get the freight quote in week 1. Freight two chambers, compressors and accessory kits from Lehi to a climate-controlled Knoxville storage unit (10 by 10 is enough for four kits), not the representative's home (stock at a home may create a "physical location" for licensing; ask counsel). Keep two in reserve for the first installs; order more only after the third LOI.
10. **Tracking.** Add Tennessee rows to the admin portal only when an agreement is signed; until then the prospect workbook is the system of record. Add a "Status", "Last contact" and "Next step" column to the Prospects sheet and update it daily.

## 3. Phase 1: who to contact first, and in what order (weeks 5 to 12)

The list has 263 prospects. Do not work it top to bottom. Work it in the order below. Waves overlap: start wave 2 in the third week of wave 1, and so on, so that every named prospect gets three touches (visit, call, email) over three weeks without stalling the pipeline.

**Ranking logic.** First, businesses whose customers already pay $75 or more for recovery services and that have a treatment room, with named owners ahead of unnamed ones. Second, performance gyms with a coach on the floor. Third, sports-medicine practices and pro clubs, which need a medical lead's blessing. Fourth, colleges, which have trainers and budgets but slow approvals. Fifth, everything else. Schools go last because they cross a season, need insurer approval and need the health systems that employ their trainers. Anything with "Yes" in the Hyperbaric on website column is skipped.

**Geography.** Work in clusters so a day's visits are within a few miles: (A) West Knoxville and Bearden along Kingston Pike; (B) Farragut and Hardin Valley; (C) downtown, North Knoxville and Powell; (D) Maryville and Alcoa; (E) Oak Ridge and Clinton. Clusters A and B hold most of wave 1.

## 4. Phase 2: from interest to install (per prospect)

1. **Day 0, interest.** Send the Tennessee LOI and the agreement the same day. Log it.
2. **Day 1 to 7, site check.** Photos or a video walk-through: 4 by 8 feet clear, grounded outlet, no flame or heat source in the room, temperature control, access path for a 60 lb chamber bag and a compressor.
3. **Day 7 to 14, approvals.** Business sends its COI (additional insured: the entity named on the agreement, settled in Phase 0) and landlord consent. Business confirms lease, zoning and fire code. If the fire marshal's general answer from Phase 0 was conditional, the business asks its inspector in writing using the one-page description.
4. **Day 14, sign.** Definitive Agreement signed; first month plus $200 collected by Stripe or ACH; sales tax applied.
5. **Day 14 to 21, install.** Representative installs, trains two staff, runs a session with them, leaves the runbook, consent forms and pricing suggestion, completes the checklist, takes photos.
6. **Day 28, first check-in.** Session count, questions, menu wording check (no "therapy").
7. **Day 60, renewal conversation.** Session count, revenue, staff feedback, upgrade to a longer term.

## 6. Weekly report and decision points

Weeks below count from the start of Phase 0; outreach days count from the first outreach call (about week 5).

**Week 4 (end of Phase 0):** Gates 1 to 4 complete? If counsel or a fire marshal has blocked air-only business placements, or has not answered, stop and reassess before any outreach.

**Week 9 (outreach day 30):** Pipeline check: at least 60 touches logged and two site checks.

**Week 13 (outreach day 60):** Three LOIs targeted. Fewer than two means the pitch or the list is wrong; review objections before continuing.

**Week 17 (outreach day 90):** First installs in; review freight and labour cost per install against the line-item budget.

**Week 21 (outreach day 120):** Six LOIs and three installs targeted. Decide: expand (second representative, concentrator upgrade if cleared, home rentals if licensed), hold (keep three placements, no new outreach), or exit (remove equipment, keep the list warm).

**Outreach day 180:** Score sessions per chamber (month 2 of each placement) and first-term renewals.

## 7. Costs to track

The $15,000 cap is provisional until the two quotes that drive it (counsel and freight) are in during week 1. Build the budget as line items before committing: Tennessee counsel (quote), freight Lehi to Knoxville for two kits (quote), storage unit (about 5 months), representative day rate and commission (rate and days per week written down), insurance endorsement, registrations ($300 minimum plus franchise and excise filings), pamphlet printing, 865 number, travel for Michael's one visit for the first install. Record each in the workbook's Summary sheet so the day-120 decision uses real numbers.

---

## 8. Rules that do not bend during the pilot

- No Tennessee placement until Phase 0 items 1 to 4 are done.
- Air-only unless counsel and the fire marshal clear the concentrator in writing.
- No home rentals in Tennessee.
- No under-18 use without the insurer's written confirmation, a parent's signed consent and a parent present.
- Purebaric runs no customer sessions itself in Tennessee; the business's trained staff do.
- "Recovery session" or "wellness session", never "therapy", on any menu, sign, post or pamphlet.
- Every regulatory comment from a landlord, insurer, inspector or official is reported the same day and filed.
- The prospect workbook is updated the day of every contact.
