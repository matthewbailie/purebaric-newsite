## How this plan works

Each **Day** is one outing from downtown Knoxville and back: drive to the first business, hand the pamphlet to the owner or manager and talk for about ten minutes, drive to the next, and so on, then home. The time shown for a day is the whole trip, door to door, and every day fits inside about 2 hours. The number of businesses differs by day because the cap is on time, not on visits: a day in a dense corridor fits eight or nine quick stops; a day with longer drives fits fewer.

**Example, Day 1.** 5 businesses: about 21 minutes to the first stop, 5 x 10 minutes of conversation, about 23 minutes driving between stops, and about 20 minutes home. Total about 1 h 54 min.

**Best bets** are the businesses most likely to say yes, judged by what they sell: contrast and cold-plunge studios, cryotherapy, float, sauna, IV lounges, stretch and recovery studios, and performance gyms, with a point added for a named owner and for a reviewer's pick. Days 1 to 10 each include at least one best bet and are ranked by best bets per hour on the road, so start at Day 1. The fill-in days after them have no best bet and can wait.

Not visited on these days: college athletics departments and pro teams (email them first; list below), businesses too far away for a two-hour round trip (listed as far trips), businesses with no usable street address (listed so an address can be found), schools, businesses that already offer hyperbaric, and businesses the research marked as unverified, closed, a weak fit, or a weight-loss or hormone clinic (listed with the reason at the end).
