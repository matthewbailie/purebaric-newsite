## How this plan works

Each **Day** is one outing: a handful of businesses close enough together to visit in a single trip of one to two hours, handing the pamphlet to the owner or manager and talking for about ten minutes at each one.

The number of businesses is different on each day because every day is capped at about 2 hours, not at a fixed number of visits: a day in a dense shopping corridor fits eight or nine quick stops, a day with longer drives between stops fits fewer.

**Worked example, Day 1.** You visit 6 businesses. That is 6 x 10 minutes of conversation (60 minutes) plus about 50 minutes of driving between them, so the outing takes about 1 h 50 min from the first door to the last. Driving from home to the first stop and back is extra.

**Best bets** are the businesses most likely to say yes: recovery studios, IV lounges and performance gyms, especially where the owner's name and a specific talking point are known. Every day starts at its best bet; the other stops are good fits or businesses on the way. Do the days in order; Day 1 is the strongest.

Not included: schools (approach the athletic director by email), businesses that already offer hyperbaric, rows the research flagged as unverified or closed, and rows without a street address. They are listed at the end. Visit mid-morning or mid-afternoon and carry the pamphlet for that type of business.
