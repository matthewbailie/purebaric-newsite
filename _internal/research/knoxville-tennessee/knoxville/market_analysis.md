# Knoxville market-attractiveness analysis for Purebaric

Prepared 10 October 2026, cross-checked against purebaric_knoxville_hbot_providers.xlsx the same day. Area: 25 miles around downtown Knoxville. Most statistics exist only for the 9-county MSA or single counties; each figure says which. Comparison markets: Phoenix/Scottsdale, Austin, Nashville.

## Verdict

Knoxville scores **62/100**: good for a small pilot, not yet proven for a full launch. The active-lifestyle base is strong (6,959 Knoxville Marathon registrants in 2026 [[16]](https://www.wate.com/sports/knoxville-marathon/covenant-health-knoxville-marathon-weekend-draws-over-7000-runners/amp/)), medical anchors are real, and the field looks open: no chamber-rental business serves Knoxville (the nearest two are in Middle Tennessee) and five self-pay providers are confirmed. Weaknesses: household incomes about 24% below Purebaric's home county, little visible hyperbaric buzz, and a 1,500-mile supply line. Recommendation: a 3-chamber pilot with business placements signed before shipping. **On the same rubric the Wasatch Front scores 73/100.** The gap comes mostly from income, proven demand and logistics.

## Five reasons to enter

1. **No local rental competitor.** The closest Tennessee renters are in Nashville and Franklin, quoting $1,495 to $2,850 a month on their rate pages (one homepage says plans from $555 a month; unconfirmed); Reclaim already rents in Utah.
2. **Endurance demand.** The marathon weekend had 6,959 registrants [[16]](https://www.wate.com/sports/knoxville-marathon/covenant-health-knoxville-marathon-weekend-draws-over-7000-runners/amp/), the Track Club has about 2,000 members [[17]](https://www.runningusa.org/industry-news/knoxville-track-club-completes-strategic-acquisition-of-knoxville-youth-athletics/), and the most-visited national park (11.5M visits) is next door [[19]](https://www.planetware.com/2127533/these-us-national-parks-experienced-most-visitors-2025/).
3. **Wellness HBOT is thin.** Five self-pay providers for a metro of 968,000, and the only one publishing prices (Bell) runs at 1.3 ATA for $100 a session [[34]](https://bellfamilychiro.com/hyperbaric-oxygen-therapy-knoxville/), so Purebaric's 1.5 ATA chambers at about $17 a session on a home rental are a step up on both pressure and price.
4. **Older buyers who spend less on housing.** 19.4% of the MSA is 65+, against 12.3% in the Salt Lake City MSA [[1]](https://citypopulation.de/en/usa/metro/28940__knoxville/) [[4]](https://citypopulation.de/en/usa/metro/41620__salt_lake_city/). The cost-of-living index is 86.4 [[13]](https://eteda.org/data-library/demographics/cost-of-living): a longevity audience with spare income.
5. **Sports and medical scale.** Neyland Stadium seats 101,915 and its 70,500 season tickets sold out [[46]](https://utsports.com/news/2025/4/29/football-season-tickets-sold-out-for-third-straight-year-with-70-500-purchased). Three hospital HBOT programs [[31]](https://www.covenanthealth.com/parkwest/services/hyperbaric-center/) already educate patients; the metro grew 7.2% [[1]](https://citypopulation.de/en/usa/metro/28940__knoxville/).

## Five reasons against

1. **Lower income.** Median household income is $74,222 in Knox County against $97,494 in Salt Lake County. 36.6% of Knox households earn more than $100k, against 48.8% in Salt Lake County [[9]](https://stacker.com/stories/7932/highest-earning-counties-tennessee) [[10]](https://stacker.com/stories/7934/highest-earning-counties-utah).
2. **Weak search demand.** Google Trends puts Knoxville's hyperbaric interest at a rounded index of 1 against Salt Lake City's 6 over five years (two recent weeks were at 55 to 60%, with most weeks below the reporting threshold), with awareness-stage queries rather than cost or "near me" queries; no local news, Facebook group or Restore-type chain (google_trends_check.md).
3. **Few studios to place chambers in.** Two recovery studios (Sessions, ThermoDynamic) already own chambers; no float or Restore studio; three IV lounges [[36]](https://lantern.llc/b/vital-hydration-knoxville/book). The prospect list has 263 rows, 88 of them recovery studios, IV lounges and med spas after the October re-sweep.
4. **Distance.** About 1,545 miles straight-line [[59]](https://wingie.ae/en/flights/salt-lake-city-to-knoxville-slca-tysa); every delivery and repair needs a contractor.
5. **Compliance and slowing growth.** 9.25% sales tax on rentals [[62]](https://www.avalara.com/us/en/taxrates/state-rates/tennessee/counties/knox.html); FDA clearance is prescription-only for mountain sickness, limiting claims [[64]](https://fda.innolitics.com/device/K072757); county growth has slowed yearly since 2022 [[2]](https://hoodline.com/2026/07/knoxville-s-moving-boom-turns-into-a-population-slowdown/).

## Recommended entry model

- **Sign before shipping.** Get letters of intent from two businesses at $783 to $979 per chamber per month. Candidates:
  - a recovery or IV clinic that already sells cryotherapy and red light, such as Vital Hydration [[36]](https://lantern.llc/b/vital-hydration-knoxville/book);
  - a sports chiropractor or physical-therapy clinic in West Knoxville or Farragut;
  - CrossFit Knoxville (West Knoxville) [[30]](https://www.crossfit.com/gyms/united-states/south/tennessee) as a third option.

  Skip Bell Family Chiropractic, Sessions Wellness Center and ThermoDynamic Health, which already have chambers.
- **Ship 3 chambers by freight:** two for business placements and one for home rental and demos.
- **Local contractor** (a Knoxville-based representative who installs and services, with a named backup); keep a spare compressor locally. The package is the same as Utah (chamber, compressor, oxygen concentrator, mask kit).
- Home rentals are offered (owner decision, 10 Oct 2026); the home-medical-equipment licence question in legal_blockers_summary.md stays with counsel.
- **Compliance.** Certificate of authority and tax registration before the first invoice; collect 9.25%; wellness claims only.
- **Marketing.** Google search ads, one digital billboard flight (about $2,500 for 4 weeks) near Turkey Creek, Track Club co-marketing.
- **Decision gate.** Both business chambers rented at list after 90 days: add 2 to 3. Otherwise bring them home.

**Three facts to verify in person**
1. Will two Knoxville businesses commit at $783 to $979 a month? Bell Family Chiropractic's published $100 a session ($70 to $80 in packages) sets the local ceiling; confirm KnOxygen's unpublished rate (one review implies about $360) in a call.
2. Is there a reliable local delivery and repair contractor, and at what cost per visit?
3. Is the field really open? Walk Farragut, Bearden and Maryville wellness clinics for unlisted mild-HBOT or rental operators.
