## Knoxville metro on its own
- Four terms compared (hyperbaric chamber, hyperbaric oxygen therapy, hyperbaric near me, hyperbaric rental): every week is 0 except five isolated spikes in five years; 5-year averages 1, 0, 1, 1. "Interest by city" returns "not enough data". Even the broad term "hyperbaric" alone shows only six spike weeks, all since late 2024.
- "hyperbaric rental" has no reportable data in Knoxville, Salt Lake City or Nashville.
- Rising related queries in Knoxville: "hyperbaric chamber benefits" (Breakout), "hyperbaric oxygen therapy" (+130%), "hyperbaric oxygen chamber" (+50%), "what is a hyperbaric chamber" (+50%). These are awareness-stage questions.
- Rising related queries in Utah: "how much does a hyperbaric chamber cost" (+200%), "how much is a hyperbaric chamber" (+180%), "what does a hyperbaric chamber do" (+200%). These are purchase-stage questions. Nashville's include "hyperbaric chamber near me" (+60%).
- Tennessee statewide rising queries include "summit to sea hyperbaric chamber" (an equipment brand) and "hyperbaric oxygen therapy near me". Both states show a Jan to Mar 2025 surge driven by news ("hyperbaric chamber explosion" is a Breakout query in Tennessee, Utah and Nashville), not by demand.

## Reading
1. Utah searches for hyperbaric chambers at roughly twice Tennessee's per-capita rate, and the Salt Lake City metro at several times Knoxville's. On the most recent weeks, where Trends has enough data to compare, Knoxville runs at about 55 to 60 percent of Salt Lake City's index.
2. Knoxville is a mid-pack Tennessee metro for this topic (third of six), behind Chattanooga and Nashville, which both have hyperbaric wellness studios.
3. The shape of the related queries is the sharper signal: Knoxville is asking "what is it and what are the benefits", Utah is asking "how much does it cost". Purebaric's Knoxville marketing would need to do education that the Utah marketing does not.
4. Nobody in either state searches for hyperbaric rentals at a reportable level. The rental category has to be created through placements, referrals and local presence, not captured from search.
