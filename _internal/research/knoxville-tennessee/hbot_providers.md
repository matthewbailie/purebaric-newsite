## Observations

- **Single-session price:** the only business-published wellness price is Bell Family Chiropractic at $100 per 60-minute mild (1.3 ATA) session ($125 first visit). KnOxygen is about $360 per session by one patient review (off-label, hard chamber). Hospital programs show $482 (Tennova Turkey Creek) to $497 (Tennova Clinton listing) as MDsave prepaid cash visits. Statewide MDsave range is $482-$525.
- **Packages:** Bell is the only published package: 10 for $800 ($80 each) and 20 for $1,400 ($70 each). No memberships or 40-packs surfaced; ThermoDynamic advertises a $199/month general membership with unclear HBOT inclusion.
- **Wellness vs medical:** 12 verified or mostly verified providers: 5 wellness/self-pay (Bell, KnOxygen, ThermoDynamic, Sessions, Redeem) and 7 medical (Parkwest, UT Knoxville, UT Sevierville, Turkey Creek, Tennova Clinton, Methodist Oak Ridge, Limb Preservation). Another 7 PT/chiro/integrative clinics appear only on Fresha and are unverified. Only 1 of 5 wellness providers publishes prices, so pricing is thin.
- **Geography:** wellness supply clusters in West Knoxville (Kingston Pike, Deane Hill, Sherlake Lane) and Hardin Valley, with satellites in Lenoir City (KnOxygen) and Oak Ridge (ThermoDynamic). Hospital HBOT is at Parkwest/Turkey Creek (west), Alcoa Hwy (south), Oak Ridge, Clinton and Sevierville. No provider found in Maryville/Alcoa, Powell/Halls (except unverified Fresha PT listings), or Farragut beyond Turkey Creek.
- **Notable:** no 2025-2026 openings or closures were reported in searches. Limb Preservation Center added HBOT around 2024 and KnOxygen shares its suite. Parkwest is run with Mobile Hyperbaric Centers. Yelp's Knoxville HBOT top 10 is padded with out-of-area businesses (Corbin KY, Morristown, Ringgold GA, Chattanooga), which suggests the true Knoxville wellness HBOT market is small (about 5 businesses). No local renter or dealer exists; the nearest renters are in Nashville and the nearest dealer is in Chattanooga, so a Purebaric rental model would face little local competition.
