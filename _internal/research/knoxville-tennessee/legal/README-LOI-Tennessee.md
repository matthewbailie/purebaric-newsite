## What it does
A non-binding term sheet that leads into the existing Business Rental Agreement and Liability Waiver (the "Definitive Agreement"). Only Section 7 binds: confidentiality, each side's own costs, no announcements or advertising before installation, a wellness-only and no-revenue-reliance acknowledgment, independent-contractor status, Tennessee law and Knox County venue, and counterparts. Everything else (equipment, term, fees, responsibilities, steps, conditions, optional reservation) is intent only.

## How it differs from the Utah version
- **Taxes (1.5).** States that Tennessee taxes equipment rentals and that 9.25% (Knox County) is added unless the business provides a resale or exemption certificate. Blount and Anderson counties are 9.75%; adjust when the location is outside Knox County.
- **Configuration (1.1).** Air-only only. The oxygen-concentrator option is not offered in Tennessee until counsel and the fire marshal clear it in writing.
- **Conditions (3).** Adds: Purebaric's Tennessee registrations (certificate of authority for the contracting entity, sales and business tax registration, and city and county business licences only if Purebaric has a Tennessee location), Tennessee insurance and local service capacity, a written fire-marshal answer that Purebaric itself obtains for the installation's jurisdiction (Knoxville applies the 2024 International Fire Code, whose §609 requires hyperbaric facilities to be maintained to NFPA 99, and the building code's §425 sends their construction to NFPA 99 chapter 14), the business's lease, zoning and landlord confirmation, and counsel's confirmation that no Tennessee or federal requirement bars renting the air-only configuration to a business. The letter promises a written go-ahead when those are met and warns of a longer lead time.
- **Tax (1.5).** States that applicable Tennessee sales and use tax is added, without a rate, because whether the local portion applies to every monthly payment is unresolved.
- **Governing law (7.6).** Tennessee law and Knox County venue for the LOI itself, so a local business can sign without a foreign-forum objection. The Definitive Agreement keeps its own clause (currently Utah law, Utah County). Tennessee courts have declined to apply a foreign choice of law to a release involving a minor (*Blackwell v. Sky High Sports*), so expect counsel to revisit that clause for Tennessee.
- **Expiry (6).** Eastern Time.

## Before sending the first one
0. Settle the contracting party with counsel. The LOI and the rental agreement name "Matthew Bailie, doing business as Purebaric"; the legal memos and the registration plan assume Purebaric LLC. A sole proprietor cannot obtain a foreign-LLC certificate of authority, and an LLC that is not a party gets no benefit from the release or the liability cap. Use one name on the LOI, the agreement and the insurance certificate.
0a. Prepare a Tennessee version of the rental agreement before the first signing: Exhibit A must list the air-only configuration (concentrator "not supplied"), the agreed term and its rate with an early-exit rule, and the same contact details as this letter. The LOI says the agreement controls on any conflict, so without that version the air-only choice and the term rate disappear at signing.
1. Decide the configuration offered in Tennessee. The legal memos (`../legal_state_tennessee.md`, `../legal_federal_local_knoxville.md`) flag the oxygen concentrator and the fire code as the two blockers for business placements. The LOI's Air-only checkbox exists so a placement can proceed without oxygen if counsel advises it.
2. Have a Tennessee attorney review this LOI and, more importantly, the Definitive Agreement's release and indemnity against *Copeland v. HealthSouth* (Tenn. 2018) before Tennessee use.
3. Confirm with counsel the Board of Pharmacy "wholesale distribution" question for renting Rx-labelled devices to businesses (open in the state memo).
4. Tennessee's 2024 automatic-renewal rule may apply to the month-to-month continuation; the LOI already states the continuation and the 30-day cancellation method in plain terms, which is the usual cure.
5. Fill Schedule 1 from purebaric.com/pricing on the day you send it; rates change in the admin portal.
