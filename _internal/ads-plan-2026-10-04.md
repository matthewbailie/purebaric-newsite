## Is business targeting on Google a good idea? Yes, as a small add inside the same budget.
A business rental is worth ~5× a home rental ($979+/mo vs $529–729) and the business page + `/book?type=business`
now exist. Business-intent searches are rare but cheap to own; one ad group sharing the existing $16/day costs
nothing extra. Keep it narrow (phrase/exact only) so it cannot swallow the budget.
