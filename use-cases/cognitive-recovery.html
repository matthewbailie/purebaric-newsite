Why home matters here

A passive intervention,
timed to your evening.

01

Use it as your wind-down

Many renters do their 60-minute session in place of late-evening screen time. Quiet, dim, breathing only.

02

For people who can't sit at a clinic

Post-concussion symptoms, light sensitivity, motion intolerance, screen fatigue, make clinic logistics a fresh injury. Home removes the logistics.

03

Passive, no exertion required

Sessions ask nothing of you physically or cognitively. Lie down, breathe, rest. For low-energy days, that's the point.

04

Stack with other modalities

Pair with sleep tracking, neurofeedback, vision therapy, or whatever else your protocol involves. The chamber doesn't crowd them out.
