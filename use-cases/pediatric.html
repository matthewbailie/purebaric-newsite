For families: a few practical answers

The questions parents
always ask first.

Protocols vary enormously between physicians and conditions. What follows is logistical, not medical, confirm any protocol with your provider.

Can a parent be inside?

The chamber is sized for one adult at a time. For young children, parents typically sit alongside the chamber and talk through the window port. For older children comfortable solo, parents stay in the room.

Multi-month rentals

Families pursuing long protocols usually book the 6-month or 1-year rate. We offer custom multi-year pricing, ask if that's where you're headed.

Can siblings use it too?

Yes. Once the chamber is in your home, anyone in the family who has cleared our standard medical screening can use it.

Purebaric chambers are provided for general wellness and relaxation only. Not a medical device, not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease. Always confirm protocols with your physician.
