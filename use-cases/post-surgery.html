Why renters choose home

Built for the part
where moving
is the hard part.

01

Daily, consistent sessions

Recovery protocols ask for sessions 5–7 days a week. Easy at home. Brutal at a clinic.

02

In your own room

No transfers in or out of vehicles. A caregiver can stay nearby. Setup happens around your bed and mobility constraints.

03

Caregiver-friendly

If you have help at home, spouse, family, home health, they can be in the room with you during sessions.

04

Whenever you can

Pain and sleep don't keep clinic hours. Run a session at 6am, 11pm, or in the middle of a quiet afternoon.

Suggested protocol

A typical post-op
hyperbaric regiment.

Every recovery is different. The pattern below is what many post-surgical renters land on, confirm with your surgeon or PT before starting.

5–7×
Sessions per week

Daily sessions in the first weeks after surgery, dropping to 3–5×/week as you stabilize.

60 min
Per session

Standard mild hyperbaric protocol. Read, rest, or sleep, most renters find it easy to fit into the day.

6–12
Weeks

Most surgical renters book a 2- or 6-month rental. Longer recoveries often go to one year.

Purebaric chambers are provided for general wellness and relaxation only. Not a medical device, not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease. Always confirm protocols with your physician.

Clinic vs. home math

40 sessions, 40 drives.
