About Purebaric

Why we built this in Utah

Purebaric exists for one reason: to make a meaningful hyperbaric protocol possible in your own home, on your own schedule, at a price that makes sense for the way mild hyperbaric therapy actually works, daily, consistently, over weeks.

Founder

Matthew Bailie

Matthew has spent years building products that actually meet the needs of the people who use them, software, services, and now hyperbaric chamber rentals. Purebaric is the same instinct, applied to a category that needed it.

The clinic model breaks the math of hyperbaric. A meaningful protocol asks for daily sessions over weeks, a cadence almost no one can sustain at $80 to $300 a visit, plus the drive and the scheduling. So Matthew built Purebaric around the only configuration that actually works: the chamber lives in your home, you use it whenever fits your day, and we handle delivery, setup, training, and pickup.

The promise is simple: what we measure is what we promise. Every chamber pressure-tested before delivery, every concentrator verified at 95 to 99.9% oxygen, every customer treated like the conversation matters more than the sale. Connect with Matthew on LinkedIn.

The equipment

About the chamber we rent

We rent the Purebaric Soft-Shell 1.5 ATA, a portable mild hyperbaric chamber engineered specifically for residential use.

Each chamber is paired with a high-purity oxygen concentrator that delivers 95 to 99.9% oxygen through a mask during sessions. The chamber walls use a thicker TPU material chosen for reliable pressure performance at Utah elevations (4,000 to 7,000+ feet), where lower ambient atmospheric pressure means the chamber works harder to reach its target.

Our chambers are inspected and pressure-tested before every delivery. We provide complete operating instructions, a printed quick-reference guide, and personal onboarding during the install, not just a manual and a wave goodbye.
