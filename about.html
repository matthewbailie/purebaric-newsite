About Purebaric

Why I built Purebaric

Purebaric is a small, local business based in Lehi. I rent professional-grade 1.5 ATA mild hyperbaric chambers to homes across the Wasatch Front and Park City, and I deliver, set up, and support every one of them myself, so you can do the daily sessions hyperbaric actually calls for without driving to a clinic.

Founder

Matthew Bailie

When I had COVID, I lost my sense of smell. After living with a reduced sense of smell for years, I finally decided to try hyperbaric sessions. I spent an hour in a chamber five days a week. About a month later, my sense of smell returned. I was ecstatic!

The sessions, however, were very expensive, and traveling to a clinic nearly every day was hard to fit around my schedule. I kept thinking about how much easier the experience would be if the chamber could come to me.

That idea became Purebaric.

I provide mild hyperbaric chambers for your home, so you can use the chamber at your convenience. It's a much better service, at a lower price. I deliver the chamber, set it up, and show you how to use it. When your rental is finished, I return to pick it up.

Purebaric is a small, local business based in Lehi, serving the Wasatch Front, Park City, and the surrounding counties. If you have questions about hyperbaric chambers or want to talk through the process before deciding, please reach out through the contact page.

The equipment

About the chamber I rent

I rent the Purebaric Soft-Shell 1.5 ATA, a portable mild hyperbaric chamber built specifically for use at home.

Each chamber is paired with a high-purity oxygen concentrator that delivers 95 to 99.9% oxygen through a mask during sessions. The chamber walls use a thicker TPU material chosen for reliable pressure performance at Utah elevations (4,000 to 7,000+ feet), where the thinner air means the chamber has to work harder to reach its target pressure.

I inspect and pressure-test every chamber before it goes out, and I set it up with you in person, so you're comfortable running a session on your own before I leave. You also get complete written instructions and a printed quick-reference guide.
