The process

From reservation to pickup

  1. 1

    Reserve your chamber online

    Fill out the reservation form and provide your rental length. Most reservations are confirmed the same day.

  2. 2

    We coordinate delivery and payment

    We reach out to schedule delivery and collect payment, then help you choose the best room: at least 8' × 5' of floor space, near a power outlet, away from heat sources.

  3. 3

    In-home setup and training

    We deliver, set up, and pressure-test the chamber on site. Hands-on training takes about an hour and covers safe operation, mask use, session protocols, and what to do in an emergency.

  4. 4

    Begin using the hyperbaric chamber

    Use the hyperbaric chamber whenever it's convenient for you. Most people do 60 to 90 minute sessions, 3 to 7 times a week. Our team provides support throughout your rental, and a printed quick-reference guide stays with it.

  5. 5

    We pick it up

    On your last day we come collect the chamber. If you want to keep going, you can extend or schedule a future rental at preferred rates.

Requirements

What you'll need in your home

Three simple things, most bedrooms and home offices already qualify.

Space

8' × 5' minimum

The chamber itself is about 36" × 89" (3' × 7.5'). You'll want extra room to enter and exit comfortably. Bedrooms and home offices are most popular.

Power

Standard outlet

A 110V outlet within 8 feet. The compressor and oxygen concentrator together draw roughly the power of a window AC unit, no special wiring needed.

Access

Door & stair clearance

30" minimum doorway. We handle stairs within reason, just let us know when you book and we'll plan for it.

Ready when you are

Reserve your chamber and we'll take it from there. Delivery, setup, and training are included.
