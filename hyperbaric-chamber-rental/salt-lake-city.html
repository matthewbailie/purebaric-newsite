Salt Lake City rental FAQ

Do you deliver hyperbaric chambers in Salt Lake City?
Yes. Salt Lake City and the rest of Salt Lake County are in our core delivery area. Delivery, setup, hands-on training and pickup are included in every rental.

Usually, yes. You need about 8 by 5 feet of floor space, a standard 110V outlet within 8 feet and a doorway at least 30 inches wide. Let us know about stairs, elevators or building rules when you book.
