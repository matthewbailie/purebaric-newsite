Park City rental FAQ

Do you deliver hyperbaric chambers to Park City?
Yes. Park City, Deer Valley, Kimball Junction, Heber and Midway are in our delivery area. We confirm mountain delivery details, like winter road access, when you book.

Yes. The chamber uses reinforced soft-shell construction with dual pressure gauges and is built to hold reliable pressure at Utah elevations, including mountain towns.

Yes. Rentals run any length from 1 to 12 months, so you can time it to a season. Longer rentals cost less per month.
