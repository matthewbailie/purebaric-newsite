Draper and Sandy rental FAQ

Do you deliver to Draper, Sandy and South Jordan?
Yes. Draper, Sandy, South Jordan, Riverton, Herriman and the rest of the south valley are in our core delivery area.
How much space do I need?
About 8 by 5 feet of floor space, a standard 110V outlet within 8 feet and a doorway at least 30 inches wide. The chamber itself is about 36 by 89 inches.
How much does it cost?
Home rentals run from $729 a month down to $529 a month on a 12-month rental, with unlimited daily sessions. Delivery, setup, training and pickup are included.
