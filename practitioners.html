A referral program
that actually serves your patients.

For chiropractors, naturopaths, functional medicine MDs, PT clinics, orthopedic surgeons, concussion specialists, and others who want to recommend home mHBOT but can't store, sanitize, or staff a chamber themselves.

01

Referral kits, in your branding

One-pagers explaining mHBOT for your patient base, co-branded with your clinic. Use them as a handoff at the end of an appointment.

02

Tracked referrals

Each practitioner gets a referral link. We attribute every renter to your clinic. Reports go out monthly so you see what you're sending us.

03

Compensation

We offer either a flat referral fee per qualified rental or a recurring percentage of the rental, whichever fits your clinic's policy. Always disclosed to the patient.

How it works

The whole onboarding usually takes one phone call and a signed referral agreement.

  1. 1

    Intro call

    30 minutes. We learn about your practice, your typical patient, and which conditions you'd refer for. You ask anything.

  2. 2

    Practitioner agreement

    One-page agreement covering scope, compensation, patient disclosure, and clinical communication. Reviewed by your counsel if you'd like.

  3. 3

    Materials & link

    We send your referral kit, a unique link, and a co-branded patient PDF. You can hand the PDF to a patient at the end of an appointment.

  4. 4

    Ongoing communication

    We let you know when one of your patients books, when their rental starts, and (if they consent) anything they share with us about outcomes.
