The math at home

A year at home costs about the same as one clinic protocol

In Utah, walk-in mild hyperbaric (1.3 ATA) sessions run $80 to $150 at wellness clinics. A typical protocol asks for 3 to 5 sessions a week, and the costs add up fast.
