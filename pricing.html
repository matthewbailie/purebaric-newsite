Pricing

One simple rule: the longer you rent, the less you pay

Every rental includes delivery, professional installation, hands-on training, sanitization, pickup, and a team you can call or text. No purchase, no maintenance, no charge until we confirm your delivery.

For your homeFor your business
Why rent

Everything a chamber owner gets, without the price of owning one

Buying a chamber means a large upfront payment, then storage, upkeep, and resale when you are done. Renting gives you the same unlimited daily access for one monthly rate, and we handle the rest.

All-inclusive, one rate

Delivery, setup, pressure test, training, sanitization, and pickup are in the monthly price. Nothing is added on.

Unlimited daily sessions

Use it every day, on your schedule. At home that works out to about $8 a session at two sessions a day on a 12-month rental.

Rent longer, pay less

Every step up in rental length lowers your monthly rate, up to 27% off at home and 20% off for businesses.

No risk to start

Requesting a chamber is free. We confirm delivery to your ZIP first, and you can cancel for a full refund up to 7 days before delivery.
