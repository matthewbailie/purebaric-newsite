How to fix it

iPhone
  1. Use Safari (not Chrome).
  2. Use an iPhone 13 Pro or later on iOS 18 or later.
  3. Go to Settings > Apps > Safari > Advanced > Feature Flags and turn off “Prefer Page Rendering Updates near 60fps”. Then force quit and reopen Safari.
  4. Turn off Low Power Mode.
Android
  1. Use Chrome.
  2. Set the screen to its highest fixed refresh rate. On a Pixel: Settings > Display > Smooth Display. On a Samsung: Settings > Display > Motion smoothness. Names vary by phone.
  3. Turn off Battery Saver.
  4. 144 Hz phones fail, because 144 is not a multiple of 40. Set the phone to 120 Hz if it offers it.
