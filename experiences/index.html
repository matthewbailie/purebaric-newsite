40 Hz Light & Sound

A soft amber glow and gentle music that pulse together 40 times a second. 40 Hz is in the “gamma” range of brain-wave activity. Start it on your phone and let it run while you rest.

Why it’s getting attention

Researchers at MIT and other labs have been studying whether 40 Hz light and sound could support the brain in Alzheimer’s disease and dementia. In mouse models it was linked to less amyloid and tau buildup and better memory. In small, early human studies it was safe and well tolerated, with preliminary signs worth following up.

Still early. Most of the evidence is from mice, and the human studies are small. It has not been shown to treat, slow or prevent Alzheimer’s or dementia in people. We offer it as a calming, research-inspired experience for relaxation.

The research

  • Iaccarino et al., Nature, 2016 40 Hz light flicker lowered amyloid in Alzheimer’s mouse models. Mice.
  • Martorell et al., Cell, 2019 Light plus sound reduced amyloid and tau and improved memory in mice.
  • Murdock et al., Nature, 2024 Helped the brain’s waste-clearing pathways remove amyloid in mice.
  • Chan et al., PLOS ONE, 2022 Early human study: safe and well tolerated; 15 people with mild Alzheimer’s showed preliminary, exploratory signs. Small trial.

Uses flashing light. Not for anyone with photosensitive epilepsy or seizures triggered by flashing lights. The page checks your screen first and only turns the light on if it can show it accurately. Sound-only mode is always available.
