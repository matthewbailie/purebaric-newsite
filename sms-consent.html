SMS Consent

Purebaric SMS notification consent

This page is public and requires no sign-in. It shows exactly how a Purebaric administrator opts in to text-message alerts, for anyone who needs to verify our SMS program.

Last updated: September 26, 2026 (consent wording v2)

Purebaric sends transactional website-notification text messages only to the Purebaric owner or another authorized administrator. These notifications alert an administrator when the Purebaric website receives:

  • A new reservation request
  • An out-of-area reservation request
  • A new customer review
  • A weekly reminder that rentals are ending within the next 10 days (a count and the earliest end date, with a link to the admin portal; it contains no customer details)

Customers who submit these forms do not receive messages through this SMS program. Reservation requests and reviews are submitted by the general public; the text alerts described on this page go only to the administrator who enrolls their own number in the admin portal.

How an administrator opts in

  1. The administrator signs in to the password-protected Purebaric admin portal.
  2. The administrator opens the Notifications page.
  3. The administrator enters the mobile number that will receive alerts.
  4. The administrator selects the notification categories they want to receive.
  5. The administrator affirmatively checks an unchecked SMS-consent checkbox.
  6. The administrator selects Enable SMS notifications or Save SMS settings.

The consent disclosure shown before enrollment states:

"By checking this box, I agree to receive transactional SMS alerts from Purebaric about new reservation requests, review submissions, and rentals that are about to end. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. See our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy."

SMS enrollment is optional. Administrators can continue using the Purebaric admin portal without enabling text notifications.

How to opt out

An enrolled administrator can stop SMS notifications by:

  • Replying STOP to a Purebaric text message; or
  • Disabling SMS notifications in the Purebaric admin portal.
