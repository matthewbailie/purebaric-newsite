Why Utah Altitude Makes Home 1.5 ATA Hyperbaric Especially Useful for Athletes

Published on September 16, 2026 by Purebaric

How high elevation affects arterial oxygen concentration, and why pressurized mHBOT provides a competitive edge for endurance athletes in Salt Lake & Park City.

Breathing higher concentrations of oxygen under mild pressure (about 1.3 to 1.5 ATA) raises the amount of oxygen dissolved in blood plasma, in addition to the oxygen your red blood cells already carry. In higher-altitude places like Utah, where ambient air pressure is lower, some athletes use mild hyperbaric sessions at home as part of their recovery routine.

What some athletes look for

  • Oxygen in the plasma: under pressure, oxygen dissolves directly into blood plasma, not only onto hemoglobin.
  • A consistent routine: a daily routine is easier to keep up when the chamber is at home rather than at a clinic.
  • Convenience at altitude: sessions fit around training without the travel.

A common routine is 60 to 90 minutes per session. Individual experiences vary, and research on mild home hyperbaric use is still developing.

This article is general wellness information, not medical advice, and describes mild hyperbaric use at 1.3 to 1.5 ATA, which differs from higher-pressure clinical HBOT; findings from clinical studies may not transfer. Individual results vary and are not guaranteed. Purebaric chambers are not medical devices. Talk with your physician before starting, especially if you have a health condition.
